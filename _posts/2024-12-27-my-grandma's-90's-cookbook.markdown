During the holidays my grandma showed me this old book of recipes.

It's in portuguese and really worn out with oil stains (I suppose from using it while cooking).
 
Some of the recipes have interesting names, like Torta Besta ("Dumb Pie"),
Beringelas "Tchan" ("Zing!" Eggplants), and Bolo D'água (Water Cake).

It also showed me how important and personal writing can be for people, and made me want to preserve it, so I decided to save it here.
